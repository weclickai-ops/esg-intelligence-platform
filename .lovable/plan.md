# ESG Advocacy Corporate Website

## Goal
Build a premium, responsive ESG Advocacy homepage that presents the company as an AI-first intelligence, research, and strategic impact partner—not a conventional consultancy or publication.

## Visual direction
- Follow the selected **Editorial instrument** composition: disciplined intelligence grid, strong editorial hierarchy, large whitespace, fine rules, and deep-green contrast sections.
- Use the locked **Forest & Porcelain** palette: `#063F2E`, `#0B5D43`, `#F7F8F4`, and `#D8DDD6` through semantic design tokens.
- Use **Space Grotesk** for headings and **DM Sans** for supporting text.
- Integrate the uploaded ESG Advocacy logo as the primary identity and derive a matching favicon from it.
- Use custom abstract data visuals, charts, signal maps, and interconnected systems instead of generic environmental photography.

## Homepage structure
1. **Navigation** — logo, required navigation links, prominent consultation action, and a compact mobile menu.
2. **Hero** — exact headline and supporting copy, two requested actions, and an animated ESG intelligence signal visualization.
3. **Future of ESG** — an interactive, data-led transition model explaining the move from compliance to purpose-driven value.
4. **Fu-Tech system** — an interconnected visual model linking future technology, AI, research, governance, trust, innovation, and impact.
5. **Solutions ecosystem** — five expandable solution disciplines containing every supplied service, without becoming a basic list.
6. **Why ESG Advocacy** — five distinct cards with concise explanations and restrained animated states.
7. **Industries** — all twelve audience groups in a precise, scannable grid.
8. **ESG Intelligence hub** — editorial cards for research, reports, intelligence, future-of-ESG work, expert perspectives, case studies, and thought leadership.
9. **Vision and mission** — two high-impact editorial panels using the supplied statements.
10. **Final consultation section** — exact closing message and a strong conversion action.
11. **Footer** — restrained brand closure and section navigation.

## Interaction and responsiveness
- Add measured scroll reveals, signal pulses, chart motion, solution expansion, and subtle pointer interactions.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and keep animation secondary to clarity.
- Preserve the desktop editorial composition while reorganizing grids, navigation, and data visuals for tablet and mobile.
- Connect navigation and calls to action to real page sections; consultation actions will lead to the contact area on this initial frontend-only version.

## Technical details
- Replace the current placeholder homepage and add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Add font links in the document head rather than importing remote fonts through CSS.
- Store the uploaded logo through the project asset flow; create a small optimized local favicon from the same identity.
- Keep colors, typography, spacing, borders, and motion centralized in the global design system.
- Use accessible semantic sections, one H1, descriptive image text, keyboard-operable controls, and visible focus states.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, check interactions, and resolve any build or runtime errors.

## Scope
This phase builds the complete public-facing homepage experience. It does not add appointment scheduling, content management, user accounts, or persistent contact submissions.
