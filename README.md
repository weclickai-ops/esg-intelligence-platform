# ESG Intelligence Platform

Create a premium, modern, highly sophisticated corporate website for “ESG Advocacy”.

Brand:

ESG Advocacy

Tagline: Purpose. Progress. Impact.

Positioning: AI-First ESG Intelligence, Research & Impact Platform.

Use the uploaded ESG Advocacy logo as the primary brand identity. Build the visual language around the logo’s deep forest green, white, and subtle neutral tones.

IMPORTANT:

This must NOT look like a blog, news website, generic NGO website, or traditional consulting website.

The website should feel like a combination of:

- Premium ESG consulting firm

- AI / technology intelligence platform

- Global research institution

- Strategic advisory company

- Thought-leadership brand

Overall visual direction:

Minimal, premium, intelligent, futuristic, editorial and trustworthy.

Use generous whitespace, sophisticated typography, subtle animations, elegant data visualizations, abstract ESG/technology visuals, glass/soft-card elements where appropriate, and strong visual hierarchy.

DESIGN SYSTEM:

- Primary color: deep forest green inspired by the logo

- Background: predominantly white/off-white with occasional deep green sections

- Typography: premium modern sans-serif with an elegant editorial secondary typeface if appropriate

- Large bold headlines

- Thin lines and subtle borders

- Rounded cards but avoid excessive “startup SaaS” styling

- Sophisticated micro-interactions

- Smooth scroll animations

- Subtle parallax/gradient effects

- Interactive data/ESG visualizations

- High-end corporate photography only where genuinely useful

- No cheesy environmental stock imagery

- No excessive icons

- No crowded sections

- No blog-like homepage layout

RESPONSIVE:

Design desktop-first but make the entire experience excellent on tablet and mobile.

UX:

The website should immediately communicate:

“ESG Advocacy is a serious, future-facing ESG intelligence and AI-powered strategic partner.”

Make every section visually distinct and purposeful.

Prioritize conversion toward “Book an ESG Consultation”.

The final website should feel like a premium global intelligence company rather than a conventional ESG consultancy.
